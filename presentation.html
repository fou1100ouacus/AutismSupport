⏱️ Section 2: Problem Statement (2 min)

The Real-World Problem

  • Daily overwhelm - Mothers lack structured tracking for their child's daily patterns
  • Maternal isolation - No safe community for sharing experiences
  • Information overload - Complex medical advice vs. need for simple, practical tools
  • No central hub - Scattered resources without daily routine support
  • Safety concerns - Risk of unsafe online spaces and unmoderated content

Current Gap

🎯 Objectives & Scope

⏱️ Section 3: Objectives & Scope (2 min)

Main Objectives

  • Provide daily tracking for child's behavioral patterns
  • Create safe, moderated community for mothers
  • Offer bilingual support (Arabic/English)
  • Implement AI-powered gentle insights (non-medical)
  • Reduce maternal isolation through peer support
  • Build mobile-first, accessible platform

Project Scope

✅ In Scope

  • User authentication & authorization
  • Child profile management (one child per mother)
  • Daily tracking system
  • Safe community with moderation
  • Video upload for motion analysis
  • Abilities assessment tracking

❌ Out of Scope

  • Medical diagnosis or treatment
  • Multi-child support
  • Telehealth integration
  • Professional directory

Design Patterns
